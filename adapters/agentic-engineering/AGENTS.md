## Authority

Adapters translate provider-native behavior into Bettor evidence. They do not own shared procedure, correctness truth, repository admission, merge, release, secrets, or permission widening.

```text
shared port law
→ Bettor immutable binding
→ provider adapter
→ normalized evidence
→ Bettor native/domain gate
→ Bettor receipt
```

Provider output is never promoted directly to consumer PASS.

## Safety and license rules

- Preserve the repository's Apache-2.0 core licensing.
- MIT and Apache-2.0 providers may be integrated under their notice/attribution obligations.
- Kenn Forge (Elastic-2.0) and Ghosthub (AGPL-3.0) remain external-process/API integrations by default.
- Do not vendor restricted provider source, bypass notices, or infer a broader commercial right from tool availability.
- Do not expose provider credentials, local databases, session tokens, private paths or raw private reasoning in receipts.

## Writer and DAG rules

One active Worker owns one adapter path/resource lease. Path-disjoint adapters are siblings. A child exists only when it consumes named unmerged bytes/contracts. One convergence owner updates aggregate indexes and closure docs after adapter leaves are stable.

## Stop conditions

Stop on missing immutable shared binding, unknown/changing provider license, stale provider identity, missing exact-subject receipt, provider output being treated as correctness truth, overlapping writer leases, private-data egress expansion, or any merge/release/permission action not admitted by the owning policy.
