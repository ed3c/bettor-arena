# Rollback boundary

Before composition selection, rollback is the removal or reversion of this terminal leaf's owned paths:

```text
loop_wiki/loopx-worker-gateway/**
.arena/modules/loopx-worker-gateway/**
.github/workflows/loopx-worker-gateway.yml
```

Generated modular projections may be regenerated by the repository sync workflow; they are not hand-edited. Rollback does not delete evidence from already published PR checks and cannot be performed as a production action by an Agent.
